## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Did I lock the door? Did I turn off the stove? Did I already take my pill?

If you've ever walked back home just to check, or lain awake wondering, this app is for you. One glance and you know: yep, you did it.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap a card the moment you do it, like "Locked the door".
• Later, when the doubt creeps in, open the app: "Today at 8:42 AM · 2 h ago".
• That's it. No more going back to check. No more guessing.

WHY PEOPLE LOVE IT
✓ One tap to log. Faster than unlocking your phone's camera.
✓ Clear answers: see exactly when you last did each thing.
✓ Daily reminders: "Did you check the stove?" at the time you choose.
✓ Undo button for accidental taps.
✓ History by day, so you can look back over the week.
✓ Make your own checks with 20 icons and 8 colours.
✓ Beautiful light and dark mode.
✓ Works offline. No account, no sign-up.
✓ Private: your logs never leave your phone.

PERFECT FOR
• Locking the front door, the car or the garage
• Turning off the stove, the oven or the iron
• Taking your medicine or vitamins
• Feeding the dog or cat
• Closing windows, unplugging appliances, watering plants
• Anything you tend to double-check

This app is a simple memory aid and log for everyday tasks. It is not a medical device.

Questions or ideas? Write to us from Settings → Send feedback. We read every message.

Did I lock it? Yep, you did.
```
