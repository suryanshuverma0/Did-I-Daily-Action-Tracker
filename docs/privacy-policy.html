Privacy Policy – Rest Easy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Rest Easy ("the app") is developed by Suryanshu Verma ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

The checks you create (for example "Locked the door"), the times you log them, your reminder times and your settings are stored only on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts. We never receive, see or sell this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Notifications

If you turn on a daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app performance and diagnostic data

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Security

Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Your logs never leave your device.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
